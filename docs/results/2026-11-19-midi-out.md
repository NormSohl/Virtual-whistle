# 2026-11-19: MIDI out from the model

Norm: drive synths from the physical model with his standard ±12-semitone pitch bend. One note on/off, the low
D reached by bending down the octave, so two octaves play from one held note. Breath on a CC chosen in the
panel; buttons to mute the audio and enable MIDI.

## How it works (play page)
- `web/pitch-tracker.js` listens to the model's own audio in the AudioWorklet (before the mute and limiter):
  YIN on a 5.3 ms window, once per audio block (2.7 ms at 48 kHz), 450-3200 Hz.
- Gate: opens when the output is above -42 dBFS and periodic for 2 blocks; closes below -50 dBFS, or as soon as
  the breath reaching the model drops under 0.02 (30 Pa). While the gate is open, non-periodic frames (fingering
  changes, octave breaks) hold the last pitch, so slurs never retrigger.
- MIDI: note 86 (D6) velocity 100 while the gate is open; pitch bend = model pitch - D6 over ±12 semitones
  (D5 full down, D7 full up), sent before the note-on and then on every change of 2 units (0.3 cents);
  RPN 0 = 12 semitones sent on enable. Breath = the model's breath (after the WARBL mapping and ramps),
  0-1500 Pa as 0-127, on CC 2 (default), 11, 1, 7, channel aftertouch, or off.

## Notes
- MIDI pitch is the model's pitch, so it carries Generation D's sharpness (about +20 to +80 cents vs A440).
- Note-on waits for the model to speak: 12-45 ms after breath starts, longest on soft low D.
