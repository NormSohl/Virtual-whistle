# 2026-11-01: First impedance model, generic D whistle

**Not a real instrument.** This is a model-designed D whistle used to exercise the pipeline before Norm's measurements arrive. Nothing here is validated against a real whistle yet.

## What was done
- Transfer-matrix model (`acoustics/tmm.py`): lossy cylindrical bore (Keefe 1984), side toneholes as T-networks (Keefe 1990, Dalmont et al. 2002 end corrections), unflanged foot radiation, window as an inertance. Playing pitch = impedance minima seen by the jet.
- Physics tests (`tests/test_tmm_physics.py`): the model's tube resonances agree with Benade's independent boundary-layer dispersion formula within 3 cents; registers are harmonic; holes raise pitch in the right order; closed holes flatten slightly; warmer air raises pitch ~3 cents/°C.
- Design fit (`acoustics/demo_generic_d.py`): starting from a rough 12.5 mm bore tube, least-squares adjusted bore length, 6 hole positions and diameters to the standard D chart (`data/charts/d_whistle_standard.csv`), primary non-half-hole fingerings, both registers. Result saved to `data/generic_d/geometry_fitted.json`.

## Findings
- **Diatonic notes:** all within 7 cents in both registers (rms ~3 cents). Largest residual is an octave stretch: D6 is ~13 cents wide of D5. Real whistles show octave stretch too; we'll see how it compares with measurements.
- **Hole size and position trade off.** Without a preference, the fit drove the lower holes to the 9 mm limit. A weak pull toward 6.5 mm holes (5 cents per mm) gives finger-friendly 6.0-6.7 mm holes at almost no cost in tuning. Holes 4 and 5 sit close together because F#-G is a semitone.
- **Cross-fingered C natural (oxxooo) is ~43 cents sharp.** This matches player lore that oxxooo works on conical bores but is sharp on cylindrical ones (the fingering source says the same). First sign the model captures cross-fingering physics.
- **Half-holes:** at 50% coverage the model predicts notes ~55-68 cents sharp of the semitone; it needs only ~12-20% open to land in tune. Treat as unreliable: the partial-hole model (open part as a smaller round hole) is crude, and real half-holing is a slot under a finger. Measurements will tell.
