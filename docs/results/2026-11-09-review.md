# Code review and breath-rise re-measurement (2026-11-09)

Whole-project review (Claude Fable 5.2). Fixes are in the same commit as this note; see `docs/decisions.md` for the one-line reasons.

## Breath rise: the model and the real whistle were measured differently
`tune/evaluate.py` scores how far first-register pitch rises with breath (run 6: 51 cents on D, 84 on G) against "real ~10 / ~18 cents" from `data/generation_d/breath_sweeps.md`. The two numbers are not the same measurement:
- model: lowest to highest pitch of every 60 ms frame within 30 dB of the loudest part of the sweep, from where the note first speaks to the octave break;
- real: the range of *held* notes found by `analysis.pitch` (steady within 30 cents for 0.4 s), which leaves out the soft end where pitch is falling.

The model's yardstick applied to the same recording (`2026-11-01_halfholes_cnat_sweeps.m4a`, first-register frames, 2nd to 98th percentile of pitch):

| Frames kept | D | G | B |
|---|---|---|---|
| within 30 dB of the loudest | 71 cents (574-598 Hz) | 118 cents (758-811 Hz) | 106 cents |
| within 20 dB | 31 cents | 86 cents | 89 cents |
| within 10 dB | 35 cents | 74 cents | 80 cents |

Slow decrescendos in the recording show it directly: G falls from 806 Hz to 788 Hz as the level drops 18 dB (41-42 s), D from 596 to 586 Hz (28-30.5 s).

So on a like-for-like basis the real Generation rises roughly 30-70 cents on D and 75-120 on G. Run 6 (51 / 84) is in that range; the lossless Generation D preset (12 / 34) is steadier than the real whistle. Limits of this check: loudness stands in for breath pressure (none was recorded), and the frames include note starts and ends.

Consequence: "step 2, change the jet model so pitch stays steady with breath" is not needed to match the real instrument. Steady pitch remains a fair goal for the ideal whistle, as a chosen property.

Also tested and ruled out: the jet lowpass's breath-dependent phase as the cause of the rise. Holding that phase constant (scratch copy of the JS model) did not reduce it (run 6: D 73, G 74 cents).

## Fixed
- `tune/fit_wall.py` crashed without a second argument, and scored every failure (even a missing `node`) as 1e4; it now runs one evaluation first and stops on a broken toolchain.
- Wall losses in the C++ engine; smaller delay lines; geometry checks in both engines and the impedance model.
- `acoustics.whistle.predict` started its search at 200 Hz, which would skip a bass instrument's first register; the floor now follows the bore length (unchanged for whistles).
- JS model: `jetStrouhal = 0` gave silence (no fixed-cutoff fallback as in the C++).
- Tests 22 -> 29: engine agreement with wall losses, a pin on the Generation D scale tuning (10.3 cents rms, 23.0 max), the geometry checks, the long-bore search.
- `requirements.txt`; README status.

## Checked and left alone
- Scale pitch at 48 and 192 kHz is within 1.2 cents of 96 kHz (per-sample constants in the jet stage do not move the tuning; register break points were not compared).
- Tonehole, window and foot filters match their stated formulas; raw-frame byte offsets match between firmware and page.
- In the take of 2026-11-07, covered holes read exactly closed; open holes read 96-97 % open (about a cent).

## Not done
- Firmware: entering raw-stream mode sets `useBellSensor = true` and exit does not restore it; exit does not check which host asked. Needs a rebuild and a hardware test.
- Jet-delay rate limit (`jetDelayRise`): a breath-only drop from D' to D takes up to ~130 ms to reach the new jet delay. It was added for the lossless bore's release; worth trying without it in the wall-loss preset, by ear.
- Play page: thumb, R4 and bell are still ignored; the register bands are drawn for the Generation D preset only.

## Follow-up (2026-11-10, Claude Opus 5.5)
Second pass over the review's fixes and its "Not done" list.
- Checked again and still holding: the C++/JS geometry check limits agree (2 .. 1021 samples), `init()` resets every filter and goes silent on a bad layout, `web/play.html` rebuilds byte-identical from its template, and the breath setting scales both the raw-stream and the stock-MIDI pressure paths. `tune/fit_wall.py` now catches fewer exceptions, so the extreme corners of its parameter box were run: all three return a cost and none raises, so a single bad candidate won't stop a fit.
- Firmware (`useBellSensor` not restored on exit): fixed on branch `raw-stream-bell-restore` (from `warbl2-raw-stream`). A second bug was behind it: `loadPrefs()`, which runs on any preset change or Config Tool edit, reset the bell sensor from the saved setting, so it could turn off in the middle of a stream. The decision now lives in `updateBellSensor()`, which keeps the sensor on while streaming, and exit restores the saved setting. Exit also has to come from the host that started the stream. Not compiled (no nRF52 toolchain on the Mac used); needs a build and a hardware test.
- Jet-delay limit: on the play page as "Wall losses (retuned), no jet-delay limit" for an A/B by ear. Offline, removing it shortens the breath-only drop D' -> D only from 150 to 120 ms (G 90 -> 80), so most of the lag is elsewhere; see `docs/decisions.md`.
- Still open: thumb, R4 and bell on the play page (roadmap); register bands for presets other than Generation D.
